Think of it like a scratchpad, beside my book. while I have a page open, it should be able to read the page and explain it to me - I also want to make the process of learning faster instead of me reading every line. I want to be able to understand it immediately.

I would like to make a tool that will instantly have the context of the book, previous pages, examples, formulas ideas that were discussed so that the agent can quickly help me with my problems - in case if i forgot something.
